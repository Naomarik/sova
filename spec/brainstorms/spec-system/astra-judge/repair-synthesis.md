# Repair judgment: disclose provenance, review meaning, keep scope literal

**Decision proposal only. Implementation and further experiments remain unapproved.** I compared all five `repair.md` proposals, the archived comparison inputs/results, manifest, pilot report, and prior synthesis. Decisive source/spec checks use `/tmp/sova-spec-pilot-c4d7993` (HEAD `c4d7993`), not the changed live source. Only this document was written; no pilot edits or experiments were performed.

## Decision summary

Recommend a small **manual packet repair**, not a coverage engine:

1. Say exactly which declared prose was returned. Separately disclose **provenance gaps**, without calling cited lines distilled behavior.
2. Add a short, reviewed boundary note when an inspected section contains important promises absent from the claims. Reconcile reusable promises on demand, not by importing the previous rubric.
3. Put unresolved conflicts inline; keep named frontier risks visible, with relevance conditions rather than shared-file filtering.
4. State task intent and root rationale in one sentence. Keep literal dependency closure; trim duplicated scaffolding first.
5. Label discovered tests as **candidate evidence** until their assertions are inspected. Use the same consequential-assertion check in both planning arms.

Keep portable ANY-project `.sova/spec`, foldaidev § identifiers, incremental prose authority, standalone core, and optional minor-mode consumer unchanged. Do not add task modes, source annotations, mandatory incumbent Markdown, or a second graph.

The smallest useful next evaluation, **if separately authorized**, is four held-out planning sessions, with one conditional repeated pair—not an automatic 12- or 30-session program. Do not combine the first repair comparison with simulated adoption.

## The minimum repair, precisely

### 1. Two separate statements: provenance and reviewed meaning

A mechanical inventory may report **uncited**, **partially cited**, or **cited provenance**, against a named snapshot and selected closure. Surface orientation is listed separately. Even fully cited provenance says nothing about whether every condition survives in the claim.

Why this distinction matters: including the broad surface span hides Focus outright. Excluding it helps, but does not finish the job. The `/send` citation covers lines 79–88, including placeholder, Stop, and Escape details not reproduced by its send-acceptance prose. Treating any overlapping bullet as distilled makes the same error at a smaller granularity. The shared representation claim cites line 81 without adopting everything that sentence says about acceptance and clearing. Therefore none of the proposed set-arithmetic ledgers can honestly output “distilled coverage.”

Use the inventory as an inspection aid. A brief **human-reviewed omission note** can name semantic gaps within inspected sections, stating its review boundary. Unknown source behavior remains unknown. Do not infer absence of requirements from absent citations, or suppress an entire heading because another claim cites one line.

No incumbent: say “No incumbent inventory; semantic completeness unassessed.” Claims and source/evidence review still work. Markdown headings and test imports are optional discovery aids, not portability prerequisites.

### 3. Preserve risk while reducing noise

Keep literal closure and a complete available evidence map. Remove duplicated maps and explanatory scaffolding before omitting substantive material. Frontiers may use one-line relevance conditions: “Refused-retry targeting is unresolved; a label-only edit must not alter that path.” Do not turn “outside mapped files” into “irrelevant,” or replace named risks with an opaque count. If output is staged, explicitly name the withheld items and provide authorized access; essential conflicts cannot be staged away.

Tests found through names/imports are “candidate evidence; assertions uninspected.” Only after reading the actual assertion may a reviewer say what it checks. For consequential predictions, ask what the fixture supplies and what the assertion compares. No generic metadata promises to cure reasoning failures.

### 4. Authority stays separate

Candidate status permits targeted reads; it neither mandates full duplicate reading nor makes savings impossible. Conversely, an adopted label cannot prove semantic sufficiency.

A later authority simulation needs independent review of accurate claims and **exact promises** transferred, not wholesale cited spans. Designate it “authoritative for this experiment only,” leave live authority untouched, retain other incumbent requirements, and expose unresolved conflicts. Price that review separately. A simulation measures planning behavior under that instruction, not actual adoption, stale reconciliation, or maintenance.

## Proposed minimal trial—permission required

**Preparation ceiling: three person-hours, estimated:** 60 minutes reusable packet/boundary review; 30 minutes independent task selection and rubric; 30 minutes root selection and packet preparation; 60 minutes scoring and attribution. Record actual author/reviewer minutes separately. If the ceiling is inadequate, stop preparation and report it—not unpaid hidden curator work.

Freeze reusable repair rules and reviewed notes first. An independent evaluator then chooses **two new tasks**, one narrow and one cross-boundary, excluding the original tasks and worked tasks named in these proposals. Keep rubrics hidden from the packet author. Task-specific work is root/intent selection and literal rendering, not adding rubric answers to claims.

Run two arms—incumbent and repaired candidate—with the same approved model/settings and fresh sessions: **four sessions**, maximum ten minutes each. Preselect the cross-boundary task for one possible repeated pair: **six sessions maximum**. Repeat only if the initial comparison leaves attribution or stability uncertain and no packet-integrity defect has appeared. This is a diagnostic screen, not statistical confidence.

Primary measures: named critical misses, consequential false assertions, and unreported relevant conflicts. Attribute conservatively: misleading packet, missing disclosure, root mismatch, independent reasoning, or unresolved cause. A miss despite a warning does not by itself prove disclosure useless.

Secondary measures: supplied context, retrieved prose, source, mixed/unclassified output, word compliance, elapsed time, preparation/review minutes, and retained artifact bytes. Count supplied packets once in totals, including their actual delivery; distinguish later rereads. Record file/range boundaries at retrieval time. Echo markers without reliable metadata cannot retrospectively split mixed output. Physically exclude prohibited directories. Keep the same word-count rule across arms.

**Stop/continue rules:**

- Inaccessible essential warnings, fabricated semantic-coverage labels, false authority transfer, or confirmed misleading packet assertions stop that packet variant immediately.
- An independent planner misread is a failed plan, not proof the graph is unsafe. Use the preselected repeat where warranted; do not repair an evaluator answer into the packet between repeats. Unknown attribution stays unknown.
- A clear repeated critical-quality regression, no useful quality/cost advantage, or excessive preparation cost means stop expansion and retain the manual fallback.
- Consider requesting minimal tooling only after no known packet-integrity defect remains, no repeated critical-quality regression appears, and measured reduced delivery or better preservation justifies preparation cost. A single noisy win or expensive hand rendering alone is insufficient. Tooling still requires approval.

This trial cannot validate cross-project portability or the reconcile/adopt lifecycle. Those remain separate authorization decisions. The present decision is smaller: make the packet’s boundary truthful and useful before asking automation to reproduce it.
