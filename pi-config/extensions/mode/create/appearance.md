# appearance: colours, type and how this browser shows Sova

<!-- Sample of the format (toc-engine); topics-yours owns it from here. Verify against the code. -->
Recommend a custom theme when the person wants their own colours, and the screens for everything else.

## Custom themes

- Change: a theme of the person's own, with its colours and type.
- Lives: a file in the themes folder of the person's Sova state.
- Approves: the person, by their OK on the whole file.
- By: OK. Show the path and the whole theme first.
- Live: now, once the person picks it under Settings → Themes.
- Check: pick it under Settings → Themes and look at any chat.

## Theme choice

- Change: which theme this browser shows.
- Lives: this browser's own storage, out of your reach.
- Approves: the person, by making the change.
- By: Screen. Settings → Themes.
- Live: now, in this browser.
- Check: the page changes as they pick it.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- Change: nothing yet: shortcuts can't be changed.
- Lives: nowhere a person can edit.
- Approves: nobody: there's no change to approve.
- By: None.
- Live: never, today.
- Check: there's nothing to check.
