# Merge round: find, judge, ask

Steps 1 to 3 of each round. Paths here are relative to the playbook's folder (`references/` is beside `PLAYBOOK.md`).

## Each round
1. **Find.** Branches reach you from the user, the Overseer or a session's message; keep them in that order. A wake that says `Reason: Ready to land: …` names the branches that just turned Ready to land. `round.mjs status` reads Sova's merge board, the same readiness Sova's sidebar shows, and lists every branch ahead of master with a worktree, sorted by what to do next, with the counts on its first line. Read its whole output, every time: never pipe it through `grep`, `head` or `tail`, which drops branches. The owners come from the board; the driver records them itself, so you don't look owners up by title or by session.
   - **Ready to land** is what you queue: the work is finished and committed at that head (Sova's own rule: no uncommitted files, no conflicts, checks not failing, no open questions, idle, and the session's last turn finished). "Ready to merge" and "Waiting for your OK" without it are not finished work: leave them.
   - **Landing specs** means the owner is promoting its spec drafts on its branch (Sova told it to): leave it. With the owner's reason it can't, the board says so and it is Ready to land with Merge Without Specs… on its card.
   - **Owner busy**: queue it on a later round.
   - **Owner archived**, or **no session tracks this worktree**: the branch is the user's call, and it has no owner chat, so no Landing card. Tell the user once (the report lists it the first time) and ask for a typed OK in your own chat ("say land <branch> here"), never "press Land It"; land it only on their word. The user naming a branch to land is that word: `round.mjs go <branch>`, then check it.
   - **Owner unknown** (Sova hasn't read every session yet, right after a restart): leave it for the next round; it is not "no owner".
   - When `status` exits 2 because the board is missing or old, say that owners are unknown and why, and land nothing that round.
   - **A release branch** (`feat/release-*`) is never yours to land, whatever its chip or owner says, and never by the user's word to you: the release playbook (`.sova/playbooks/release/PLAYBOOK.md`) publishes it with its tags in one push. While a release session says a release is in progress, land and push nothing until it says the release is published.
   `round.mjs note` is for what you learn another way (a message): it names the owner only while it is newer than the board's reading.
   Master can grow without you: an owner may merge its own branch. A branch with a worktree that is already in master (`git branch --merged master`) needs no check: `round.mjs landed <branch>` takes it from there. Commits in `origin/master..master` you didn't land get what your landings get before you push: typecheck, both test tiers (`pnpm test`, then `pnpm test:int`) and build in the main checkout. Sessions that land themselves (the `/merge` prompt) are the usual source: verify their range the same way, and never assume their own check covered the combined master.
2. **Judge.** The chip doesn't know intent. Read the owner's latest messages (`session_read`): is the work it set out to do finished, and does it have open alignment questions? Ready to merge or Waiting for your OK without Ready to land is not finished work: never merge on the chip alone. Topic notes reach you only between turns, so a hold can be sent before you land and arrive after: read the owner's latest messages again just before landing. A hold that arrives after a landing is reported to the owner and the user, never undone by you.
3. **Queue** each Ready to land branch whose owner is idle (no turn running, no queued messages, no workers working; the board says which): `round.mjs ask <branch>` reads the board again, refuses a busy, archived or stale owner, and queues it. Sova then shows the owner's session the **Landing card** itself, from your chat: you send nothing. When you tell the user a card waits, name the owner's chat by its title with the link `status` and `ask` print (`"<title>" (#/sid/<id>)`), never just "its card is showing"; say "press Land It" only for a branch whose card `status` says Sova showed, and add that "land it" typed in that chat does the same. Don't poll: go on with other branches. The user's **Land It** arrives as a message ("Go ahead: land <branch> at <commit>."), and `round.mjs status` then lists the branch under "to check": that is the OK. **Merge Without Specs…** arrives the same way, "without its specs": its check lets the drafts stay behind and names them. **Not Yet** leaves it under "not ready" until its head changes. The user's "land it" typed in the owner's chat is the same OK: that chat records it, a merge-ready wake names it (`Go-ahead typed in its own chat: <branch>`), and `status` lists the branch under "to check". So is Go on a Ready to land row of an Overseer card the user applied: Sova records it for the commit the row showed, a merge-ready wake names it (`Go-ahead on the Overseer's card: <branch>`), `status` lists the branch under "to check", and `land` names it "Apply on the Overseer's card". `status` says whether Sova showed the card, or why not; when it didn't (an open offer, say), `ask` says so, and the branch lands only on the user's word: tell the user once, asking for "land it" in the owner's chat (or "land <branch>" here), never to press a card. The user's Land It still counts after your check merges master in. A branch the user tells you to land in your chat waits for nothing: `round.mjs go <branch>`, then `round.mjs check <branch>`. A hand-back goes to the owner; once it commits the fix, the branch shows Ready to land again and you queue it at its new head.
Then check each branch: `references/check.md`.
