Logo candidates, round 2

Round 2, set K3: 4 letter-based ligatures, 2 for SOVA and 2 for TAVI, drawn after the round-1 favourite (../fable/tavi-ligature.svg — T joined to I in one stroke) was chosen. This set explores the same family without repeating it: connected initials, shared stems, a letter in negative space, continuous geometry. No owls, no branch-and-node icons, and none of the four is a redress of the round-1 marks (mine included). Every mark is one currentColor SVG on a 32-unit grid, shown in the accent as the sidebar head would use it, in ink, and reversed on the accent, at the 3 sizes the product needs. The wordmark is Inter 640 at −0.03em; the two panels are the dark and light token sets. The product name is still provisional — see ../../naming.md.

SOVA

1. Cascade

S pours into V · one stroke · currentColor · sova-cascade.svg

sova

One line, no breaks: the S is drawn from its top terminal, and exactly there the same stroke turns over and pours down into the V. The S's exit is the V's entry — the first consonant and the middle letter in a single gesture. The V happens to be the one letter both candidate names carry, and it lands on the mark's highest and farthest point.

Curved S against straight V gives the mark its rhythm: soft in, sharp out. At 16px it reads as a two-letter monogram rather than as letters spelled out, which is honest to what it is. Stroke 4, round caps and joins, no detached part to lose.

SOVA

2. Passage

S cut through O · negative space · currentColor · sova-passage.svg
sova
sova

The O is a solid disc and the S is the opening through it — the word's first letter as a channel of bare surface inside its second. The S never touches the edge; both of its terminals float inside the solid, so the letter is a passage, not a notch. The cut is true transparency: whatever the mark sits on shows through.

The only filled mark of the four, so it balances the three line marks in a sidebar row. Construction: one disc, one 3.8-unit masked stroke — the mask id is namespaced for inlining. At 16px the channel is about 2px wide and still reads as an S, which is the constraint that fixed its width.

TAVI

3. Tandem

A and V share one leg · one stroke · currentColor · tavi-tandem.svg
tavi
tavi

The pair naming.md singles out: the A's right leg is the V's left leg. One zigzag runs up, down, up through both letters, and the only added stroke is the A's crossbar laid between the two diagonals it joins. Two letters on three diagonals — nothing drawn twice.

All straight lines on quarter-unit coordinates, the crispest of the set at every size. Stroke 4.5, matching the round-1 favourite's weight. At 16px the crossbar fuses with the legs and the silhouette carries the letter sense, the same trade the fable ligature makes with its hook.

TAVI

4. Totem

T posted on the A's apex · shared vertex · currentColor · tavi-totem.svg
tavi
tavi

The opening pair composed vertically instead of along a line: the T posts onto the A and the two letters share a single vertex. The stem's round cap survives inside the apex as the joint where they meet. The mark is symmetric about the centre axis, so it sits level in a square well at any size.

Deliberately opposite in layout to the round-1 favourite, which flows horizontally; this one stacks. Stroke 4, the crossbar wide enough that the A keeps its letter sense at 24px. Nothing detached; the whole mark is one connected shape.
