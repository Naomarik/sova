# Is it good enough to land?

Start the landing reviewer with the ledger, the last round's chart and the confirm run, the winner's diff against master, the record (`node scripts/record.mjs show <name>`) and each round's Predicted vs observed table. It wasn't part of the campaign, and it gives a verdict: Land, Another cycle (with the knob to turn), or Park. Its reasons say whether the winner did what its consultant expected, and address any gain nobody expected.
