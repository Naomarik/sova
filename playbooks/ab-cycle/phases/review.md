# Is it good enough to land?

The last report goes out first, at once (`phases/decide.md`). Start the landing reviewer when the last round's outlook is saved, with the ledger, the last round's chart and the confirm run, the winner's diff against master, the record (`node scripts/record.mjs show <name>`), each round's Predicted vs observed table and the last outlook (`rounds/<n>/outlook.json`). It wasn't part of the campaign, and it gives a verdict: Land, Another cycle (with the knob to turn), Park, or Baseline kept. Its reasons say whether the winner did what its consultant expected, address any gain nobody expected, and say where they part from the outlook.
