Selected mark

The name is sova and the mark is Astra’s Fold: one stroked path, a V and an A sharing a stem, with the A’s crossbar as the only added move. Geometry is copied verbatim from ../round-2/astra/sova-fold.svg — 32-unit grid, stroke-width 4, currentColor, round caps and joins — and the same file ships as public/icons/sova-mark.svg. The colorway treatments below are borrowed from the Fable sheet, which showed them best; none of Fable’s geometry is used here. The wordmark is Inter 640 at −0.03em, per the design skill’s Logo section, and the two panels are the dark and light token sets.

One unbroken zig-zag reads as V-A; the short bar at y=17 is what stops it reading as a W. At 16 the bar is the first thing to go soft, and the mark still holds, which is the test it was drawn to pass.
