Logo candidates, round 2

4 new letter marks for the 2 names on the table, 2 each, drawn after the first round's Ligature (T joined to I in one stroke) was picked as the direction. None of these is a variant of it: each name gets one continuous-stroke ligature and one negative-space monogram, and every mark says which letters it is made of. Every mark is one currentColor SVG on a 32-unit grid, shown here in the accent as the sidebar head would use it, in ink, and reversed on the accent, at the 3 sizes the product needs. The wordmark is Inter 640 at −0.03em, per the design skill's Logo section, and the two panels are the dark and light token sets. The name is still provisional; see ../../../naming.md.

SOVA

1. Counter

S cut from O · negative space · sova-counter.svg
sova
sova

The S is the O's counter. A solid disc for the second letter, and the first letter taken out of it, so the two initials of the name share one piece of ink. The S is two three-quarter circles, which is the only S that stays round at 32 units.

The cut is 4 units wide and the rim never drops below 3, so at 16 the S is a 2px channel with a 1.5px wall around it. It is a coin, and a letter in a coin is a well-worn device; what it has over the others is that this one is a real S, not a glyph stamped on.

2. Arm

V into A · shared stroke · sova-arm.svg
sova
sova

The last two letters in one polyline: the V's right arm keeps going and becomes the A's left leg, then the A's crossbar closes it. Three strokes, one turn each, and the crossbar is the only horizontal in the mark.

At a glance it is a W with a bar, and it takes the name beside it to settle into VA. The peaks are 8 units apart and the 4.5 stroke holds at 16; the A's upper counter is 5 units tall, which is the floor for a counter at that size.

TAVI

3. Frame

A and T · one crossbar · tavi-frame.svg
tavi
tavi

The A's crossbar is the T's bar, and the T's stem drops from it to the baseline between the legs. Two letters that share one horizontal, drawn symmetric so the mark has a base and a peak.

It reads as A first and T second; the stem is what makes it a monogram rather than a letter. It is the only one of the four that is a single upright shape, which is what the sidebar head wants at 24.

TAVI

4. Inset

T cut from V · negative space · tavi-inset.svg
tavi
tavi

A solid V, and the T taken out of it. The first letter is the negative space of the third, so the two most different letterforms in the name, a bar-and-stem and a wedge, sit in one silhouette.

The walls stay above 3 units everywhere, and at 16 the T is a 2px bar and a 2px stem inside a 14px wedge. It borrows the outline of a road sign, which is candidly its risk; the rounded corners and the T keep it on this side of that.
