Design system · v1.4.0 · en-US

Fold AI Dev

The whole system, rendered — 7 foundations, 2 brand topics, and 25 components, in both themes. Use the theme toggle: nothing on these pages knows which theme it is in.

Foundations

Color

One saturated color, spent carefully.

Typography

Inter for what a person reads, JetBrains Mono for what a machine produced.

Spacing

A 4px base and nine steps.

Radius

Seven steps, assigned by element rather than by taste.

Elevation

Three steps and a flat default, in neutral black only.

Grid & responsive

The bands are the device, not a screen-size ladder.

Focus & motion

One curve, three durations, and a written rule about what never moves.

Brand

Logo

Two panels hinged at a center crease — the device seen from above.

Iconography

37 line icons on a 24px grid, shipped as local files.

Components

Button

The decision, made tappable.

Chip

A status, said twice — once in color and once in words.

Input

A 44px field with a real label.

Select & combobox

A closed list and a searchable one.

Toggle

Checkbox, radio, and switch, sharing one label row.

Card

A bounded surface with an optional head and foot.

App bar

Brand, context, and status — nothing else.

Rail & bottom bar

One navigation, two placements: a bottom bar under 768px where the thumb is, a side rail above it.

Tabs

Tabs switch views; they never submit.

Breadcrumb

Where you are, and the way back up.

Banner

An in-flow, persistent statement of fact.

Toast

A transient acknowledgment with an optional escape hatch.

Empty state

The live fact first, the absence second.

Skeleton

A placeholder shaped like the thing that's coming.

List & row

A scannable column of things to decide on.

Tree

A hierarchy that belongs to the data — a worktree's files, a scope, a set of nested groups.

Table

Aligned columns for comparing runs.

Filter bar

What a set is narrowed by, what it is ordered by, and how many it is showing — one bar, because those three facts are read together or not at all.

Meter

A measured number against what bounds it — 31 GB free of 48 GB allocated, on a 64 GB machine.

Modal, sheet & popover

Three ways to put something on top.

Tooltip

One sentence behind a term or a figure, for the user who wants it defined once.

Run timeline

A stepped record of what a worker actually did.

Diff viewer

The core review act.

Approval bar

The persistent decision affordance — where Approve, Review, and Discard live.

Chat thread

The conversation with a worker: your turns, its turns, and the tools it ran.
