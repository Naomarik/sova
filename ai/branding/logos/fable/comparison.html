Logo candidates

4 marks for the 2 names on the table, 2 each, drawn independently of one another and of any other set. Every mark is one currentColor SVG on a 32-unit grid, shown here in the accent as the sidebar head would use it, in ink, and reversed on the accent, at the 3 sizes the product needs. The wordmark is Inter 640 at −0.03em, per the design skill's Logo section, and the two panels are the dark and light token sets. See ../../naming.md for the names.

SOVA

1. Owl

silhouette · negative space · sova-owl.svg
sova
sova

The owl as one filled head. A tufted brow on top, and the eyes and beak cut out of it. The tufts are what say owl instead of ghost, and they survive at 16 because they are corners, not detail.

The beak is a 4-unit triangle. It is legible at 24 and a suggestion at 16; the mark still reads without it, which is the test it was designed to pass.

2. Gaze

two discs · offset pupils · sova-gaze.svg
sova
sova

Only the eyes. Two discs that just overlap into one shape, each with a pupil turned the same way. It is the watch metaphor with nothing around it: something is being looked at, steadily, from the side.

Reads as an owl only next to the name. Alone it is a pair of eyes, which is honest about what the product does most of the day. The pupils are 5.4 units and stay open at 16.

TAVI

3. Branch

one root · one fork · three nodes · tavi-branch.svg
tavi
tavi

A session and what it fans out into. One stem, one split, a node at each end. The join sits below centre so the mark has a base, and the three discs are the same size because no session outranks another.

It is a graph, and graphs are a crowded field for developer tools. What sets it apart is restraint: three nodes, not five, and no ring around it. The 4-unit stroke holds at 16.

TAVI

4. Ligature

T joined to I · one stroke · tavi-ligature.svg
tavi
tavi

The first and last letters of the name in one stroke: the T's stem runs down, turns, and comes back up as the I, with its dot set free. Two ends of the word tied together, the way the terminal and the browser read one transcript.

It is a monogram, so it says the name rather than the product. The dot is the only detached part and is 5.5 units wide, which is the floor for a dot at 16.
