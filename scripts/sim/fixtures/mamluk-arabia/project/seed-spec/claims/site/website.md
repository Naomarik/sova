# §site/website — The Mamluk Arabia website

A small public website for Mamluk Arabia, a traditional archery club. Visitors read what the club
teaches, see this week's classes and send the club a message. The site is static HTML today; the
club's staff cannot edit it themselves.

## §site.website/home — The home page

- The home page shows the club's name, a short welcome about traditional horse and foot archery,
  and this week's timetable: day, time and class, one line each.
- Coach Layla Haddad asked for this on the first call: "Put the timetable right under the welcome,
  parents only look for the times." The timetable sits directly under the welcome.

## §site.website/classes — Classes and prices

- The classes page lists each class with a one-line description and its price in SAR. Horseback
  archery shows "Price: on request" and says a riding assessment comes first.

## §site.website/contact — The contact form

- The contact page has one form: name, phone and message. Sending it posts to `/contact`.
- The phone number is required; Basim Al-Qahtani (treasurer) said in the March meeting: "We call
  everyone back, we never email."

## §site.website/language — Languages

- The site is in English only. Arabic is not decided yet.

## §site.website/newsletter — A newsletter sign-up

- The home page offers a monthly email newsletter: one field for an email address and a Subscribe
  button.
